I am a Staff Software Engineer focused on backend platforms, distributed systems, and financial technology. I work across teams to shape architecture, improve engineering practices, and build reliable production systems, with application security integrated into the engineering process.

My career began in backend development and grew to include application security, cloud infrastructure, and technical leadership. I approach systems end to end—from service design and data access to deployment, reliability, and security—and help teams make decisions that improve the broader platform.

## Engineering work

My experience includes payment infrastructure and platforms spanning more than 20 microservices. I have contributed to large-scale cloud migrations, query optimization, engineering standards, and cross-team technical initiatives. I focus on durable improvements to platforms and the way teams build on them, with an emphasis on shared technical direction and outcomes that span team boundaries.

My current engineering interests include:

- Backend architecture and service boundaries
- Distributed systems and service communication
- Payment and financial infrastructure
- AWS, Docker, and production operations
- Database performance and engineering quality
- Cross-team technical leadership

## Technologies

Node.js, TypeScript, NestJS, PHP, Laravel, AWS, RabbitMQ, PostgreSQL, and Docker. I also have experience reading and assessing Java and C# applications.

## Security background

Application and product security remain important parts of how I engineer. My AppSec and ProdSec experience includes secure code review, vulnerability research, and penetration testing, supported by CPTS (Certified Penetration Testing Specialist), CWES (Certified Web Exploitation Specialist), and CWEE (Certified Web Exploitation Expert) certifications.

I use that experience to make security a practical part of architecture and delivery, alongside reliability, maintainability, and product needs.
